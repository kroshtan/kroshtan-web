I'm Floris van Beers, an independent ML and AI engineer and architect. I work from Groningen under
the name Kroshtan, remotely or hybrid, and I prefer short engagements: two weeks to three months,
with a clear goal and a proper handover at the end, so your team can run what I build without me.

Most recently I was an MLOps engineer at Springer Nature, from December 2024 to September 2026. There
I worked on machine learning systems running in production at one of the world's largest academic
publishers: not the model in the notebook, but everything it takes to make that model a service that
keeps working. I built and ran services such as recommender systems and text summarisation, owned
their deployment, monitoring and reliability on GCP and Kubernetes with full CI/CD, and designed the
multi-layered ETL pipeline that fed several products, both for training and for live inference.

Before that I spent three years as a machine learning engineer at Web-IQ in Groningen, from 2021 to
2024, building web intelligence for law enforcement and online-safety organisations. I shipped
classifiers and semantic image search over large-scale web and dark-web data, and developed
multimodal video analysis that combines vision and language models. I also did project management
within the EU Horizon 2020 STARLIGHT programme and co-wrote successful funding applications,
including one to the End Violence Against Children fund. In 2021 I did a deep learning internship at
Intel, working on image segmentation.

I studied Artificial Intelligence at the University of Groningen, first the BSc and then the MSc,
and was a teaching assistant three times along the way. My research there on loss functions for
image segmentation became the publications on my [portfolio](/portfolio). I still enjoy explaining
how things work, which is why I also teach and give talks.
